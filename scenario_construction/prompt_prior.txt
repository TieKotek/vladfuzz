[CONTEXT]

You are generating natural-language navigation commands for testing Vision-Language-Action autonomous driving systems.

The test case contains two inputs:

1. A first-person driving image. The image may contain a marker labeled "TARGET" or a green target dot. This marker is only an annotation for you and is invisible to the driving model under test.
2. A structured route prior. The prior lists the ordered maneuver steps needed to reach the intended goal in the current driving scene.

[TASK]

Generate {num_commands} natural English navigation commands for the same test case. Each command should describe the intended driving task in a way a human passenger might say to an autonomous vehicle.

Use the route prior as the authoritative source for the maneuver sequence. Use the image to decide the road context and choose the most natural high-level wording for that context. The final command should sound like what a passenger would naturally say, not a literal translation of route-planner actions. If the image and route prior appear ambiguous, prefer a conservative command that preserves the route prior without adding unsupported details.

[LANGUAGE STYLE]

Write each command at the highest natural navigation level supported by the image and route prior. Do not describe your reasoning or scene interpretation.

Prefer human driving intents over low-level maneuver names:

- If the route moves from a highway or main road onto an exit ramp, say "take the exit" or "exit the highway", not "turn right at the road ahead".
- If the route follows a ramp onto a highway or main road, say "merge onto the highway" or "merge onto the main road", not just "turn left" or "turn right".
- If the route goes through a roundabout, say "enter the roundabout" and, when appropriate, "take the exit" rather than describing it as a generic turn.
- If the route stays on the same road through a bend, say "follow the road" rather than "turn".
- Use "turn left" or "turn right" mainly for ordinary intersections, T-junctions, and clearly local street turns.

Use the image to choose the most natural road-context term: highway exit, ramp, merge, intersection, roundabout, lane change, or lane following. Do not use a generic intersection command when a more specific road-context command is visually supported. Avoid template-like commands that describe only geometric motion, such as "turn right at the road ahead", when the scene supports a more natural navigation intent such as exiting, merging, following a ramp, or entering a roundabout.

[COMMAND REQUIREMENTS]

- Each command must be semantically valid for the provided driving scene and route prior.
- Generate exactly {num_commands} commands.
- Preserve the order of meaningful maneuvers from the route prior, including turns, lane changes, merges, exits, and intersection movements.
- Ignore low-level distance-only steps such as "Follow lane for 7.01 meters" unless they are needed to connect meaningful maneuvers.
- If a lane change is required, explicitly state the number of lanes and direction.
- Keep each command concise: one sentence is preferred, two short sentences are acceptable, and the command should normally stay under 45 words.
- Make the commands paraphrases of the same intended task, not different tasks.
- Do not mention that a route prior was provided.
- Do not mention the target marker, target dot, or any visual annotation.
- Do not add traffic rules, traffic light states, signs, hazards, or safety advice unless they are part of the navigation instruction itself.
- Avoid robotic wording such as "execute step one" or "according to the route description".

[OUTPUT FORMAT]

Return only one valid JSON object with this exact schema:

{
  "commands": [
    "command 1",
    "command 2",
    "..."
  ]
}

Do not wrap the JSON in Markdown. Do not include explanations before or after the JSON.
